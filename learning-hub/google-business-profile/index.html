Find your business and claim it

If you can search on Google, you can do this. Your listing is free, and it’s often the first thing customers see.

Your quick win

  • Your business found (or added) on Google
  • Your claim started, with verification on the way
  • Your correct hours and phone number showing
  1. 1

    Step 1: Log in to Google

    Use your Google account. A business one is best.

    Screenshot of this screen
  2. 2

    Step 2: Search your business name and suburb

    Like you would for any shop. Your listing is the box with your name, hours and phone.

    Screenshot of this screen
  3. 3

    Step 3: Click “Own this business?”

    Not there? Go to google.com/business and add it instead.

    Screenshot of this screen
  4. 4

    Step 4: Fill in your name, type and phone

    Use the name on your sign, exactly as customers know it.

    Screenshot of this screen
  5. 5

    Step 5: Click “Get verified”

    Google picks how it checks it’s you. You can’t choose.

    Screenshot of this screen

Try this now

Search your business on Google. Write down what’s wrong or missing. Next lesson: prove it’s you.

Words you’ll see

Business Profile
Your business’s listing on Google Search and Maps
Claim
Say “this business is mine” on Google
Verify
Prove to Google it really is your business
Service area
The suburbs you travel to, if customers don’t come to you

Waiting is normal

Google can take up to 5 business days to check it’s you. Keep going with the next course.

Stay safe

It’s free. Anyone asking you to pay to “stay on Google” is a scammer.
