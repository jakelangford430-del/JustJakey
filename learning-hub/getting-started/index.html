Getting started

Half an hour to get comfy with the basics every other lesson uses.

  • Beginner
  • Stop 0a
  • 1 lesson
  • About 30 minutes

30minutes

Your quick win

Write down (on paper, in a safe spot) the one email address you’ll use for your business. By the end of this lesson you’ll have it ready and protected with an extra login code.

Steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Get an app safely.

    Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). Search the app’s name. Before you tap Install (or Get), check who made it. Real apps list the real company.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Pick your business email.

    Use one you already have, or make a free Gmail just for the business. Use this same email for the lessons that follow.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Make a strong password.

    Three or four random words strung together is easy to remember and hard to guess. Use a different one for every account.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Turn on the extra login code.

    In your email account’s security settings, look for an option like “2-Step Verification” or “two-factor”. Follow the steps on screen. If the words look a bit different on your screen, that’s OK.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Have a look around.

    You can’t break anything by looking. Menus move, buttons get renamed. If something looks different from these steps, look for the closest match.

Try this now

Log out of your business email, then log back in. Notice the extra code step. That’s it working. Next stop: Google Chrome.

Words you’ll see

App store
Where you get apps. App Store on iPhone, Google Play on Android.
Password
Your secret word for logging in. Long and different for every site is best.
Two-factor login
An extra code (usually by text or an app) after your password. It stops strangers getting in, even if they guess your password.
Business email
One email you use only for the business, so work stays in one place

Stay safe

  • Never tell anyone a login code, not even someone saying they’re from Google, Meta or your bank. Real companies won’t ask.
  • Don’t write passwords in a text message or email.
